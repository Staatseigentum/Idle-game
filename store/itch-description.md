# Embercrown: The Ash Kingdom (0.3.0)

Build a kingdom in the dark, keep its last light alive, and rise stronger after every fall. Embercrown is a dark-fantasy pixel-art idle game about gathering embers, rebuilding a lost realm, and choosing what endures.

## Build your reign

- Stoke the embers and grow twelve distinct buildings, from the Coalpit to the Ash Citadel.
- Buy in bulk, master each building, and unlock long-term specializations.
- Explore the Lost Marches with expeditions, fragments, sieges, and conquered regions.
- Restore Crown Seals, collect and spend relics, choose relic sets, enact Royal Edicts, and push back the gloom.
- Discover Chronicle entries and make each Ash Ritual count toward the next reign.

## A new beginning

Version 0.3.0 rebuilds Embercrown with a new animated pixel-art kingdom, redesigned interface, guided tutorial, and original synthesized ambient music and sound effects. Music and effects have separate volume and mute controls. Progress continues while you are away.

Available for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android. The desktop installers include an updating launcher. The Android download is a directly installable APK.

**Save compatibility:** 0.3.0 starts a new save. Your 0.2.x save remains stored separately and is not overwritten, but it cannot be imported into the rebuilt progression.

Embercrown is still in active development. Feedback and bug reports are welcome in the comments.
