Embercrown is an idle kingdom-builder about starting over.

You tap for your first coin and buy a farm. The farm earns while you sleep. Sixteen holdings later you are running a Celestial Observatory, the map has run out before your ambition did — and then the Blight comes, as it always does, and takes all of it except what it taught you.

Eighteen ages sit between a single tent and the Embercrown itself. You will see all of them more than once.

> **Heads up: this is a very early build (0.1.0).** The art, the balance, the systems and the overall shape of the game are all still moving. Please read the "Very early in development" section further down before you start.

## New in 0.1.0

- **The Blight is no longer silent.** Once it's available, Verfall corruption visibly creeps across your kingdom's skyline the longer you leave it — a small, capped production penalty grows with it. Never forced, always your call when to embrace it.
- **The Council Hall.** A new currency, Influence, buys permanent upgrades: cheaper holdings, faster Influence, more Chronicle Points from the Blight, and a ward against the corruption above.
- **Wonders.** Three one-time mega-builds, one for each age that has no holding of its own, bought outright with gold for a permanent, powerful bonus.
- **Heirs.** Every Rebirth now crowns a new heir — a procedural name, a small trait, and a little heraldic emblem, so each life carries its own identity.
- **A Verfall Omen.** A tappable, risky sighting that only appears while the Blight is spreading: a coin flip between a short boon and a short curse.
- **The Chronicle.** The realm writes its own history now — falls, rebirths, achievements and the Blight's worst moments, logged automatically as you play.
- **Deeds and Synergies.** One-time quests with gold rewards, plus seven building synergies that reward pairing complementary holdings.
- **Dragon sightings.** A rare, tappable production buff.
- **The music reacts.** A second, dissonant layer quietly rises under the theme as the Blight's corruption builds.

## The loop

- Tap for gold, buy **holdings**, and let them earn while you are away.
- Lifetime gold carries you through **18 ages**, from Camp to the Embercrown — each with its own skyline and its own line in the chronicle.
- **16 holdings** unlock as you climb: Farm, Fishing Hut, Woodcutter Camp, Quarry, Mine, Market Stall, Tavern, Forge, Trading Post, Library, Alchemist's Lab, Wizard Tower, Temple, Treasury, Dragon Hoard, Celestial Observatory.

## What the fall is for

Nothing here is meant to be kept. It is meant to be spent.

- **Embrace the Blight** to let the kingdom fall, and turn everything you built into **Chronicle Points**.
- Spend those in the **Academy**, which raises production permanently.
- Survive **five Blights** and you unlock **Rebirth** — let the Chronicles themselves fade, and what remains becomes **Legends**.
- Legends fund three **Dynasty paths**: **Steel** (every holding produces more), **Magic** (the Academy's teachings cut sharper), **Cunning** (every Blight pays out more). Only the active path applies, so pick one and live with it.
- The **Council Hall** spends a second currency, **Influence**, earned passively from trade holdings, on permanent upgrades.
- **10 achievements**, each a permanent bump to production.

## Features

- **Offline progress** while the game is closed, capped at 8 hours — and switchable off if you would rather not have it.
- **Export and import your save** as plain text, so a run can survive a reinstall.
- **English and German.**
- **No art assets and no audio files.** Every sprite is composed and dithered in code, and the music and sound effects are synthesised at runtime.
- **Windows, macOS and Linux.** The launcher updates itself before it starts the game.

## Very early in development

Embercrown is a 0.x build. I would rather over-warn than surprise you:

- **The design is not settled.** Layout, art direction, pacing, the prestige math — any of it can change, and some of it will. Things you like now may look different or be gone entirely in a later build.
- **Balance is a first draft.** Numbers are being tuned as I go.
- **Saves can break.** A save is one JSON blob, and if a future version changes its shape in a way the game cannot read, it falls back to a fresh start rather than a broken one. Use the export button if a run matters to you.
- **Features are still landing.** Systems that exist today may be reworked before they are finished.

If that sounds acceptable, I would genuinely like you to play it. Early feedback is worth more to me right now than polish is.
