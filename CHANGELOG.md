# Embercrown changelog

## 0.3.0 — The Ash Kingdom

This is a substantial rebuild of Embercrown around its original idle-kingdom idea.

- A new dark-fantasy pixel-art kingdom, animated buildings, larger scenes, and a redesigned desktop and mobile interface.
- A guided first run highlights the next action, teaches gathering, construction and the first mastery, and can be skipped or restarted.
- Twelve buildings with bulk purchases, three mastery tiers and long-term specialization paths.
- The Lost Marches: expeditions, fragments, sieges, conquered regions and relic-set choices.
- Crown Seals, relic powers, Royal Edicts, Omens, the Beacon, the Ash Ritual and Chronicle discoveries add longer-term goals.
- An original synthesized ambient theme and action sounds, with separate music/effects volume and mute controls.
- Fixed doubled percent signs in the new game's English and German descriptions.
- Desktop builds for Windows, macOS and Linux, plus Android. Audio uses the platform's native/JVM output and fails safely when no audio device is available.

**Save compatibility:** The rebuilt game starts with a new save. Version 0.2.x saves are kept separately and are not overwritten, but are not imported into this new progression.
